隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 10 月 26 日 · 适用于 布光 Bulight for iPhone / iPad

这件事很简单：布光不收集任何数据。不设账号、不连服务器、不集成任何统计或广告 SDK。

布光（以下简称“本 App”）由个人开发者 Lei Zhao 开发。本政策的写法刻意不用法律套话，因为这件事本身很简单。

我们不收集任何数据

本 App 不设账号、不设登录、不连接我们的服务器。以下内容全部只存在于你的设备上：

  • 你调好的灯光参数、保存的光配方
  • 白纸校准得到的色偏补偿值
  • 你拍下的照片

卸载 App 后，上述数据随 App 一起从设备中删除。我们没有任何途径取回，也没有任何副本。

相机

相机用于两件事：自拍补光时的取景，以及白纸校准时读取纸面颜色。

  • 相机画面不会离开这台设备，不上传、不录制、不缓存到相册以外的地方
  • 只有你按下快门时，当前照片才会写入系统相册
  • 白纸校准时读取的像素值只用于当场计算补偿量，计算完成后即丢弃

相册

本 App 只申请“添加到相册”权限，不申请读取整个相册。因此我们无法看到你已有的照片。

内购

完整版通过 Apple 的 App 内购买完成。支付与退款全部由 Apple 处理，我们不会接触也不会保存你的任何支付信息。本 App 只记录一个“是否已解锁”的本地标记。

第三方 SDK

没有。本 App 不集成任何统计、广告、推送、崩溃上报 SDK。

儿童

本 App 不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不收集任何年龄信息。

变更

若未来版本引入需要联网的功能，本政策会先行更新，并在 App 内以显著方式提示。

ENGLISH SUMMARY

Privacy Policy — Summary

Bulight is made by an independent developer, Lei Zhao. This summary explains the same policy in English. The Chinese version above is the governing text.

We collect nothing

Bulight has no account, no sign-in, and does not talk to any server we operate. Your light settings, saved recipes, paper-calibration offsets and photos exist only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them. We have no copy and no way to retrieve them.

Camera

The camera is used for framing self-portraits and for reading a sheet of white paper during calibration. Camera frames never leave the device — nothing is uploaded, recorded, or cached anywhere outside your photo library. A photo is written to the system photo library only when you press the shutter. Pixel values read during calibration are used for that one calculation and then discarded.

Photos

Bulight requests add-only access to your photo library. It cannot see or read the photos you already have.

In-app purchase

The full version is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Apple. We never see or store your payment details. The app only keeps a local flag recording whether the unlock was purchased. Restore Purchases is available at any time on a new device.

No third-party SDKs

None. No analytics, no advertising, no push, no crash-reporting SDK is bundled.

Children

Bulight is not directed at children under 13 and collects no age information.

Changes and contact

If a future version ever needs network access, this policy will be updated first and the change announced inside the app. Questions: itzhaolei@foxmail.com
